# Road to the Championship (YES THIS IS A.I, PLACEHOLDER DESCRIPTION) 🏃

A cross-country team management and simulation game built in Java.

## About

**Road to the Championship** puts you in the role of a high school cross-country coach. Your objective is to build a competitive program, develop athletes, manage resources, and lead your team to a state championship.

Every decision matters. Recruitment, training intensity, athlete development, fatigue, and team morale all influence your team's performance throughout the season.

## Core Features

- **Athlete Recruitment** — Discover and recruit prospective runners to build your roster.
- **Team Management** — Manage your athletes and evaluate overall team strength.
- **Training System** — Develop athlete fitness through different training intensities.
- **Fatigue & Recovery** — Balance training workloads to avoid overtraining and declining performance.
- **Season Progression** — Navigate a 12-week competitive season.
- **Championship Competition** — Put your team's development to the test.

## Technology

- Java
- Object-Oriented Programming
- JavaFX (planned for the graphical interface)
